About Us - Down the River

Our mission is to provide unforgettable white water rafting experiences while promoting adventure, teamwork, safety, and respect for nature. We believe every river journey is an opportunity to create amazing memories!

History

Down the River was founded in 2010 by adventure lovers. What started as a small local adventure company has grown into a place where people from all over the world can experience the excitement of exploring rivers and nature.
